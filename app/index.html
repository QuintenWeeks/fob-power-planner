Effect on this design

Model (U), Scioletti et al. (2017)

Mixed-integer linear model: buy generators, batteries and panels and dispatch them hour by hour at least cost. The paper's nonlinear model (P) has a quadratic fuel curve (set to linear, af=0, since maker data are linear), battery power = voltage × current, and SOC × current in battery aging. (U) linearizes binary×current exactly (24a-e) and SOC×current with a McCormick under-estimator (25a-h).

Added for this project: three load tiers with lost-power penalties, a host-nation grid with outages, equipment already owned at zero cost, representative-day blocks (battery starts each day at 50% and must end at least there), and an N+1 generator rule.

Solution approach (paper Section 3): heuristic H screens hundreds of candidate designs with the nonlinear battery equations, then (U) is solved to a 2% gap for the four best generator/battery choices with solar size free.
